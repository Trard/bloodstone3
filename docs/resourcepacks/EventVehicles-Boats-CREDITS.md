# Boats and keys

Boat and ferry schematics supplied by the user. Ferry windows are fitted between the original slabs. Block textures are referenced from Minecraft; existing resource pack credits remain intact.

The three flat 16x16 key textures were drawn and exported with Blockbench MCP. Editable projects are in `docs/keys/`.

## Engine recordings

- Motorboat: [River boat with outboard engine](https://freesound.org/people/nomadas/sounds/690160/), nomadas, 2023-06-10, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Recorded on a powered river boat on the Loire with a Sony PCM-D50.
- Ferry: [Diesel engine 1.WAV](https://freesound.org/people/Sandermotions/sounds/328615/), Sandermotions, 2015-11-13, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Recorded beside a running ferry diesel engine with a Zoom H1.

Public HQ MP3 previews from Freesound are retained as sources. The pack uses 1.08-second excerpts starting at 80 and 8 seconds respectively, converted to mono 24 kHz Ogg Vorbis, filtered below 55 Hz and above 3.8 kHz, normalized to 0.75 peak and faded over 80 ms at each end. Playback follows the engine position every second, overlaps the boundaries and changes pitch with speed. No generated sound is used for these engines. Previous recordings remain in the source directory but are not packaged.

The previous Mixkit 3077 recording remains in the source folder for history and is not packaged as the boat engine in this version. Its saved license remains alongside the source.
